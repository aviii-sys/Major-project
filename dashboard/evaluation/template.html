AProject overview

What the project is about, where the data come from, and what exists today.

Research problem and objective

Problem
Flash floods on the Guadalupe River rise within hours. Forecasting the river stage 15 minutes to 3 hours ahead from gauge history is hard when floods start from rain that the gauge cannot see yet.
Study site
Objective
Test whether a hybrid Transformer–LSTM improves multi-horizon stage forecasting and rapid-rise anticipation beyond well-evaluated GRU, LSTM and classical baselines, under sparse extreme-event data, and whether this can support early warning.
Forecast horizons
15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours (stage change at the gauge)
Evaluation standard
Storm-grouped, chronological cross-validation with an embargo; official NWS stages kept separate from experimental thresholds.

Proposed final architecture Code written · no trained result

The model code exists and passes unit tests, but a first training run was stopped after 10 of 24 stability runs, so no hybrid has been trained to a result. The comparison it must beat (GRU, LSTM, ridge, damped trend, XGBoost) is complete.
